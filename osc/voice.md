# voice.md for Old School Contracting

This is the voice profile for Old School Contracting LLC (OSC), an excavation
outfit in Chattanooga, TN. Every skill in this pack reads it when
`~/.instagram-agent/voice.md` does not exist. To change OSC's voice on one
machine only, copy this file to `~/.instagram-agent/voice.md` and edit that
copy. That copy wins over this one.

This repo is public. Keep prices, customer names, addresses, account IDs and
anything else private out of this file.

Anything in `{{double braces}}` still needs the owner's answer. The skills
never fill one in by guessing. A draft that needs one comes back with the
braces still in it.

## Safety on camera

Every Reel and photo gets checked against this before it is approved:

- Nobody standing inside a machine's swing radius or under a raised bucket.
- No one in an unprotected trench or deep cut.
- No one riding in a bucket or on the outside of a machine.
- Seat belts on in the cab when a shot shows it.
- Call 811 before digging; say so whenever a post shows digging.
